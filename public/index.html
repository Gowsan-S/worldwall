Installation & Integration Guides

Windows 10 / 11 (Lively Wallpaper or Wallpaper Engine) ▾
1. Download free open-source Lively Wallpaper (from Microsoft Store or GitHub) or open Wallpaper Engine.
2. Select Add Wallpaper → Web / URL.
3. Paste your live K-Wall link:
http://localhost:3000/?wallpaper=true
4. Click Apply to enjoy the live updating wall as your Windows desktop wallpaper!
Mobile Phones (Android & iPhone PWA) ▾
1. Tap your browser share or menu button (Chrome/Safari).
2. Select Add to Home Screen or Install App.
3. Open K-Wall from your home screen — it runs in fullscreen standalone app mode without browser bars!
macOS (Plash or WebScreenSaver) ▾
1. Install Plash (free on Mac App Store) to turn any website into your desktop wallpaper.
2. Add your K-Wall Live URL to Plash.
3. Enable "Open at Login" for a continuous global wallpaper on macOS!
